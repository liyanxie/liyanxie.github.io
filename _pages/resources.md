This is a short collection of resources I have found useful. It is not meant to be complete in any sense. I update this page occasionally as I come across new material.

## Statistical Inference: Classical & Modern

1. [An Overview of Large Language Models for Statisticians](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.17814) — Wenlong Ji et al. _The American Statistician_, 2026.

2. [Intermediate Statistics (36-705)](https://www.stat.cmu.edu/~larry/%3Dstat705/) — Larry Wasserman, Carnegie Mellon University. Lecture notes.

3. [STATS 300C: Theory of Statistics](https://candes.su.domains/teaching/stats300c/) — Emmanuel Candès, Stanford University. Course and lecture notes.

## Diffusion Models

1. [What Are Diffusion Models?](https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2021-07-11-diffusion-models/) — Lilian Weng. Blog post.

2. [Generative Modeling by Estimating Gradients of the Data Distribution](https://yang-song.net/blog/2021/score/) — Yang Song. Blog post.

3. [Diffusion Models for Discrete Data](https://deepgenerativemodels.github.io/assets/slides/cs236_lecture18.pdf) — Aaron Lou, Stanford CS236. Lecture slides and [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mCaRNnEnYwA).

## Research Writing

1. [Ten Simple Rules for Mathematical Writing](https://www.mit.edu/~dimitrib/Ten_Rules.html) — Dimitri Bertsekas. Lecture slides.

2. [Checklist for Effectively Writing Papers in Stat-ML](https://www.stat.cmu.edu/~aramdas/checklists/aadi-paper-checklist.pdf) — Aaditya Ramdas. Writing checklist.

3. [A Student’s Guide to Writing with ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/use-cases/student-writing-guide/) — OpenAI. Student writing guide.
